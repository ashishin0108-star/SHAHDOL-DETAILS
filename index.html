About Shahdol District
(शहडोल जिला परिचय)

Nestled in the northeastern Vindhyan landscape of Madhya Pradesh, Shahdol is an enchanting blend of undulating hills, lush deciduous Sal forests, extensive coal reserves of Sohagpur, and perennial river tributaries of the sacred Son.

Originally fortified by the Kalachuris of Chedi dynasty, the district possesses monumental antiquities such as the Virat Mandir and boasts vibrant tribal communities—principally Gond, Baiga, and Kol. Renowned for ancient folklore, handwoven weaves, and the life-giving Bansagar reservoir, modern Shahdol is rapidly ascending as a pivotal developmental division in eastern Madhya Pradesh.

Magnificent 10th-century Kalachuri temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. Renowned for its lofty curvilinear Shikhara and intricate sculptures comparable to Khajuraho.
